🛡️ Cyber Security AI

Cyber Security AI is an educational defensive cybersecurity platform designed to combine AI assistance with practical security-analysis tools. The project focuses on security awareness, basic risk analysis and safe defensive practices.

🎯 Project Objective

The goal is to provide users with an easy-to-use security center where they can learn about cybersecurity and perform basic defensive checks from one interface.

Safe device security checklist

Guidance only; this page does not control your computer.

  • Turn on automatic operating system and browser updates.
  • Use unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Review installed applications and remove ones you do not recognize.
  • Back up important files and verify that recovery works.

AI Quality Check

Measure whether the configured AI responds to two safe, fixed cybersecurity prompts.

This checks availability, response time and whether a reply is returned. It is not a factual accuracy benchmark. Running it sends the fixed prompts to the configured AI provider.

Screen Snapshot Review

After you click, your browser asks which screen or window to share. One captured image is sent to Gemini for analysis; do not include passwords, private messages, or sensitive information.
